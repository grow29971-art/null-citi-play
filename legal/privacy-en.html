Chat, reports, blocks and access information Game nicknames, chat text and sending times are stored on the server and displayed to signed-in users. Chat retains up to the latest 1,000 messages for 30 days, with scheduled expiry cleanup; clients fetch the latest 100. Reports contain the reporter and reported account IDs, relevant message and nickname, reason, submission time and review outcome, retained for 30 days after submission before scheduled cleanup. Reports are not public. Block preferences remain until unblocked or the account is deleted. Chat restrictions apply until their recorded expiry. Account deletion also removes that account's messages, reports, blocks and restrictions. Other devices update displayed records on their next fetch; copies independently saved by another user cannot be recalled. Sending and reporting are rate-limited. Access, correction, erasure, restriction, consent withdrawal and deletion requests when the app is unavailable can be sent to grow29971@gmail.com.
